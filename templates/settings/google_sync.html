Phone calendar and tasks (Google)

See ALFRED's dates on your phone. Trips, bookings, career follow-ups and research review dates go to a separate "ALFRED" calendar in Google Calendar; trip reminders and packing lists go to an "ALFRED" list in Google Tasks. ALFRED can see only the calendar it creates, and syncs one way, every 30 minutes. Amounts, balances and notes stay on this PC.

Connect Google Calendar and Tasks

Uses the Google sign-in set up under Online services. In your Google Cloud project, turn on the Google Calendar API and Google Tasks API, and add both permissions to the consent screen. Disconnecting keeps the ALFRED calendar in your Google account; delete it there if you want it gone.
