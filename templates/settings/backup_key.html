Backup key (if this PC is lost)

ALFRED's backups are encrypted with a backup key that stays on this PC. So a new PC can open them, ALFRED locks a copy of the key with a passphrase you choose and keeps only that locked copy in a hidden ALFRED folder in your Google Drive. On a new PC: sign in to Google here, fetch the copy, and type the passphrase. Google cannot open it without your passphrase, and ALFRED never stores or sends the passphrase. If you forget it, the backups cannot be opened.

Backups themselves go to your Cloudflare R2 bucket (scripts/cloud_backup.py with --key-file set to the key file shown above).
