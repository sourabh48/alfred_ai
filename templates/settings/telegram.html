Telegram

Ask ALFRED from Telegram about your next trip, career follow-ups that are due and Home notices, or ask any public question. Telegram's servers see these messages, so money, documents and your saved history are never read or sent there. ALFRED only replies; it never books, sends, pays or changes anything from Telegram.

Checking…

Set up once: in Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and follow its steps. Put the token it gives you in config/local.env in ALFRED's data folder as ALFRED_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=… and restart ALFRED. Never paste the token into a chat.
