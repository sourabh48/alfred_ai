Upload Principles

The intake layer should stay consistent across modules even when the downstream dashboards differ.

  • Every important external signal should show proof.
  • Every parser should expose confidence and allow review.
  • Every long-lived cache should carry a freshness contract.
  • Recommendations should be grounded in user history, verified external evidence, or both.
