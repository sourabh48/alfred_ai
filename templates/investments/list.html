            What do these terms mean?
            Net margin
            Profit kept from each ₹100 of sales. Higher is better.
            Free cash flow to profit
            How much of the reported profit arrived as real cash. Near 1 or more is healthy.
            Debt to equity
            Borrowed money compared with the owners' money. Below 0.5 is light; above 1 is heavy.
            Current ratio
            Cash and short-term assets compared with bills due within a year. Below 1 can mean a squeeze.
            Price-to-earnings (P/E)
            Years of today's profit it would take to earn back the share price. Lower looks cheaper, but compare within the same industry.
            Volatility
            How much the price jumps around in a year. Above 40% is a bumpy ride.
            Fall from peak (drawdown)
            The biggest drop from a high point in the past year.
            200-day average
            The average price over about the last ten months. Trading above it means the trend has been up.
